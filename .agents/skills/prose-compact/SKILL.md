---
name: prose-compact
description: Rewrite written prose artifacts and agent output to be shorter, deduplicated, better structured, and more precise without losing facts, nuance, decisions, constraints, caveats, actions, or fidelity. Use when asked to compact, tighten, shorten, deduplicate, clarify, make concise, reduce verbosity/repetition, or improve prose structure while preserving meaning, especially with compact bullets, tables, or diagrams.
---

Ruthlessly cut down prose without losing any conveyed information. Preserve every distinct fact, decision, constraint, caveat, action, dependency, and open question. Deduplicate repeated ideas, replace repetitive examples with the principle they imply, disambiguate vague language, and prefer compact structure such as bullets, tables, or diagrams when it preserves fidelity with fewer words. Prefer diagrams when relationships, flows, dependencies, states, or decision trees can be shown more clearly and compactly than prose.
